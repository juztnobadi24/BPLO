Business Renewal Application

Simple step-by-step guide to register your business with ease.

Required Documents

Have these documents ready before starting:

  • ✓ Certificate of Registration
  • ✓ Community Tax Certificate
  • ✓ Sworn Declaration of Capital Investment
  • ✓ Building Permit
  • ✓ Contract of Lease (if applicable)
  • ✓ Zoning Certificate
  • ✓ Sanitary Permit
  • ✓ Fire Safety Inspection Certificate (FSIC)

Walk-in Application

Submit a photocopy of all the required documents to the person responsible at Business One Stop Shop Office

Online Application

1. Account Setup
•
Log in to iBPLS Website. Register here if needed.
•
Click New Application
2. Application Details
•
Select New for Type of Application
•
Choose Annually for Mode of Payment
•
Set Current Year for Tax Year
•
Check General Barangay Clearance
3. Business Information
•
Fill in business information
•
Click NEXT
•
Provide business operation details
•
Click NEXT
4. Business Activities
For business code, refer to PSIC Code 2019 or use search
•
Click Add Line of Business and select your business line(s)
•
Input capitalization by clicking 0.00 under Capital Investments
Upload documents (PDF/JPG only, max 10MB)
•
Click Upload Requirements, select requirement, choose file, then upload
•
Click NEXT
5. Review & Submit
•
Review all information
•
Click SUBMIT
•
Check box in pop-up and click SUBMIT again

Note: Before submitting your application, be sure to upload all the required documents otherwise your application will be declined. All applications submitted will go through the following processes below:

Verification

BPLO or BPLO Staff

Checking all the information filled out by the registrant and if all required documents were uploaded is correct.

Endorsement

Sanitary Inspector, BFP and OBO

Approval of application by the endorsing office like BFP, Building Official, Zoning Officer & Sanitary Inspector

Payment

Treasurer or Treasury Staff

One-time Assessment of Fees and Tax Order of payment will be sent to the registered email. You can proceed to payment via Online or Walk-in

Issuance of Permit

Treasurer or Treasury

The business owner/authorized may claim the official business permit at our office located at Business One Stop Shop Municipal Building, Poblacion, Liloan, Southern Leyte
